About Seasol Maritime

Our Company

A trusted maritime partner, built for India – CIS trade

Seasol Maritime India Pvt. Ltd. is a proud member of a renowned shipping and maritime group established in 2007 and based in Mumbai, India’s financial capital.

We provide freight forwarding services to customers across the CIS countries and other parts of the world – delivering comprehensive logistics solutions and seamless supply chain management, customised to enhance efficiency, reliability and value for every client.
