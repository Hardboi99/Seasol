NVOCC explained

What is an NVOCC?

A Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier sells ocean transport without owning ships. It buys space from shipping lines and issues its own house Bill of Lading to you – so you deal with one carrier, one contract, one point of responsibility.

NVOCC – containers via the Bandar Abbas gateway

We consolidate and book your cargo, issue our own house Bill of Lading and manage the sea leg and onward transit, so your shipment reaches CIS markets with one partner in charge.

  1. 1

    Enquiry & routing

    Share cargo, volume and destination – we plan the route from the Indian port via Bandar Abbas.

  2. 2

    Booking & house B/L

    We confirm space, book the container and issue our house Bill of Lading as your contract of carriage.

  3. 3

    Stuffing & consolidation

    Cargo is packed, sealed and consolidated at the Indian port with other shipments where needed.

  4. 4

    Export clearance & sea leg

    Export customs is cleared and the container sails to the Bandar Abbas gateway.

  5. 5

    Gateway handling & onward move

    At the gateway we manage transit formalities and arrange onward road or rail towards the CIS destination.

  6. 6

    Delivery & closure

    Cargo is delivered to the consignee and the job is closed with a complete set of documents.

ISO Tanks – safe, clean movement of bulk liquids

We match your product to the right tank, prepare the paperwork and follow the tank from loading to discharge – so liquids travel in a sealed, stainless-steel vessel instead of drums or bags.

  1. 1

    Cargo & compatibility check

    We review the product and its safety data sheet to confirm it suits ISO tank transport.

  2. 2

    Tank selection

    The right tank type and capacity is chosen for the product, quantity and any temperature needs.

  3. 3

    Cleaning & inspection

    The tank is cleaned and inspected, with a cleaning certificate where the cargo requires it.

  4. 4

    Filling & sealing

    The tank is filled within safe limits, sealed and weighed, and the documents are completed.

  5. 5

    Customs & dangerous goods papers

    Customs brokerage and, where applicable, IMDG dangerous goods declarations are prepared together.

  6. 6

    Transit, discharge & return

    We follow the tank to destination, coordinate discharge and arrange the empty return and cleaning.

What can go in an ISO tank

Liquid cargo, handled the right way

ISO tanks carry a wide range of bulk liquids. The right tank type depends on the product, so we check compatibility before we quote.

01

Food-grade liquids

Edible oils, juices, wine and similar products that need a clean, food-safe tank.

02

Industrial chemicals

Non-hazardous chemicals and solvents moved in bulk without drums or repacking.

03

Dangerous goods (IMDG)

Hazardous liquids in tanks rated for the cargo, with IMDG classification and declarations prepared.

04

Oils & lubricants

Base oils, lubricants and similar viscous products, with heated tanks where needed.

05

Specialty & pharma liquids

High-value liquids where cleanliness, sealing and careful handling matter most.

06

Gases & special products

Liquefied gases and other special products on request, subject to tank availability.
