Ocean Exports & Ocean Imports

Whether your cargo is leaving India or arriving at an Indian port, we run the shipment step by step so nothing is left to chance.

Ocean Exports – moving your goods out of India

From the factory gate to the destination port, we book space, prepare documents and keep your export on schedule – with a special focus on CIS markets reached through gateway ports.

  • Booking & equipment
  • Export documentation
  • Customs clearance (CHB)
  • Bill of Lading
  • Status updates
  1. 1

    Enquiry & rate quote

    Share your commodity, volume, origin and destination – we respond with a clear, route-specific quote.

  2. 2

    Booking & equipment

    We confirm vessel space and arrange the right container or equipment for your cargo.

  3. 3

    Pick-up & stuffing

    Cargo is collected, stuffed and moved to the port with the cut-off dates in mind.

  4. 4

    Export customs clearance

    Invoice, packing list and shipping bill are checked and filed through our customs brokerage team.

  5. 5

    Loading & Bill of Lading

    Your container is loaded on the vessel and the shipping documents are issued and shared.

  6. 6

    Transit & arrival updates

    We follow the shipment to the discharge port and keep you informed until it arrives.

Ocean Imports – bringing cargo into India smoothly

We follow your shipment from the origin port to your doorstep – checking documents early, coordinating customs and arranging the final delivery so your goods reach you without surprises.

  • Pre-alert & document check
  • Vessel & ETA tracking
  • Import clearance (CHB)
  • Inland delivery
  • Warehousing support
  1. 1

    Pre-alert & document check

    We review the Bill of Lading, invoice and packing list before arrival to avoid last-minute issues.

  2. 2

    Vessel & ETA tracking

    Sailing and arrival schedules are monitored, and you are told about any change.

  3. 3

    Import customs clearance

    Our customs brokerage team files the bill of entry and coordinates duties and inspections.

  4. 4

    Port handling & release

    Delivery order, terminal formalities and container release are taken care of at the port.

  5. 5

    Delivery to your door

    Cargo moves by trailer to your warehouse or plant, or into our 3PL warehousing network.

  6. 6

    Empty return & closure

    We manage empty container return and close the job with a complete document set.

What we handle

Cargo & services around every sea shipment

From standard containers to special equipment, our Freight Forwarding Division covers the full range of ocean cargo.

01

Full Container Load (FCL)

Dedicated containers for larger consignments – secure, direct and simple to track.

02

Less than Container Load (LCL)

Share container space for smaller volumes and pay only for the space your cargo uses.

03

NVOCC & ISO Tanks

NVOCC services via Bandar Abbas and ISO tank solutions for liquid and bulk products.

04

Breakbulk Handling

Heavy and oversized cargo that does not fit a standard box, planned and handled carefully.

05

Special Equipment

Open-top, flat-rack and other special equipment arranged for project and out-of-gauge cargo.

06

Customs Brokerage & Cross Trading

CHB support and cross-trade shipments that move between two countries other than India.

Our sea routes

From Indian ports to CIS markets

We connect India to Central Asia and the wider CIS through gateway ports and trusted onward transport.

Indian Ports

Cargo is consolidated and loaded at Indian ports such as Nhava Sheva (Mumbai), Mundra and Kandla.

Gateway Ports

Shipments move through gateway ports, including NVOCC services at Bandar Abbas, for onward connections.

Final delivery across Russia, Central Asia, the Caucasus and Belarus with coordinated onward transport.
