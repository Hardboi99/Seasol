Customs Brokerage & Cross Trading

Whether your goods are crossing an Indian border or moving between two other countries entirely, we manage the process step by step.

Customs Brokerage (CHB) – from filing to release

Our customs brokerage team prepares and files your documents, follows them through assessment and inspection, and releases cargo so it can move on to the airline, vessel or your door.

  1. 1

    Document collection & check

    Commercial invoice, packing list and supporting papers are reviewed for completeness and consistency before filing.

  2. 2

    Classification & valuation

    Goods are matched to the correct HS code and declared value so duty and compliance are right first time.

  3. 3

    Filing the declaration

    The shipping bill (export) or bill of entry (import) is filed electronically with Indian Customs.

  4. 4

    Assessment, duty & inspection

    We coordinate duty payment, queries and any examination of the cargo with the authorities.

  5. 5

    Out-of-charge & release

    Once cleared, cargo is released to the carrier for export or to transport for import delivery.

  6. 6

    Job closure & records

    A full set of documents is returned to you for accounts, audit and any refund or incentive claims.

Cross Trading – goods that never touch India

In a cross-trade shipment the cargo moves directly between two other countries while the booking, documentation and coordination are run through Seasol Maritime. Your buyer and supplier stay connected; we keep the paperwork and the vessel in step.

  1. 1

    Trade lane & requirement

    You share the origin, destination, commodity and timing – we confirm what is possible on the lane.

  2. 2

    Booking & routing

    Space is secured with the carrier and the routing is planned to match your sale terms.

  3. 3

    Documentation control

    Bills of lading, invoices and certificates are prepared and cross-checked so they match the contract.

  4. 4

    Coordination at both ends

    We stay in touch with the shipper, the carrier and the consignee’s side until the goods are released.

  5. 5

    Tracking & updates

    You receive clear status updates from loading to arrival at the destination port.

  6. 6

    Delivery & closure

    Documents are released as agreed and the job is closed with a complete file for your records.
