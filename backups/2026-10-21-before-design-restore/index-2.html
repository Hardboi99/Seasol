ABOUT SEASOL

Reliable Freight Forwarding & Maritime Logistics Since 2007

Seasol Maritime Pvt. Ltd. is part of a renowned shipping and maritime group established in 2007. The company provides freight forwarding services for customers across CIS markets.

Seasol focuses on comprehensive logistics solutions and seamless supply-chain management across CIS markets and other international destinations.

Its approach emphasizes customized logistics solutions that support efficiency, reliability, and value for clients.

Comprehensive Freight Forwarding

Air freight, ocean freight, breakbulk and special-equipment handling, customs brokerage, cross trading, NVOCC and ISO tank services under one forwarding division.

Cross-Border Logistics Solutions

A focus on CIS markets and cross-border connectivity, with tailored logistics solutions that support international trade.
