ABOUT SEASOL

Reliable Freight Forwarding & Maritime Logistics Since 2007

Seasol Maritime Pvt. Ltd. is a proud member of a renowned shipping and maritime group established in 2007 and based in Mumbai, India's financial capital. We provide freight forwarding services to customers across CIS countries.

Our expertise lies in delivering comprehensive logistics solutions and supporting seamless supply chain management across CIS countries and other parts of the world.

We are committed to providing customized logistics solutions that enhance efficiency, reliability, and value for our clients worldwide.

Comprehensive Logistics Solutions

We offer a range of logistics services designed to meet diverse shipping and transportation requirements, helping customers manage their supply chains efficiently.

Customized Global Logistics
