Gateways & trade corridor

From Indian ports to CIS markets

Cargo is consolidated at Indian ports, routed through the Bandar Abbas gateway and delivered across the CIS with coordinated onward transport.

  1. Indian ports

    Cargo is consolidated and loaded at Indian ports such as Nhava Sheva (Mumbai), Mundra and Kandla.

    • Nhava Sheva (Mumbai)
    • Mundra
    • Kandla

  3. Bandar Abbas gateway

    NVOCC services at Bandar Abbas connect Indian shipments to onward routes into the CIS.

    • NVOCC
    • ISO tanks

  5. CIS destinations

    Final delivery across Russia, Belarus, Ukraine, the Caucasus and Central Asia.

    • Russia
    • Belarus
    • Ukraine
    • Armenia
    • Azerbaijan
    • Kazakhstan
    • Uzbekistan
    • Kyrgyzstan
    • Turkmenistan
    • Tajikistan

Gateways listed are those named in our route planning; this is an illustrative corridor overview, not a sailing schedule.
