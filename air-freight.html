Know your chargeable weight

Airlines charge on the greater of the actual weight and the volumetric weight of your cargo. Try the quick calculator to see which one applies to your shipment.

  • Volumetric weightL × W × H (cm) × pieces ÷ 6000 – bulky, light cargo takes up more aircraft space.
  • Actual weightThe gross weight of the packed cargo, including pallets and packaging.
  • Chargeable weightWhichever of the two is higher – this is what your air freight rate is applied to.
