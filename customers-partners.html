Customer industries

Freight solutions for every industry

India’s trade with the CIS is led by pharmaceuticals, machinery, electrical equipment, chemicals and steel, alongside textiles and food products. Choose an industry to see how we move it.

Pharma & Healthcare

Time-sensitive medicines and healthcare supplies, with careful handling and refrigerated trucking for temperature-sensitive goods.

  • Air Freight
  • 3PL

Machinery & Engineering

Heavy machines, production lines and plant equipment, planned with the right equipment and secure lashing.

  • Breakbulk
  • Ocean Freight

Electrical & Energy

Transformers, generators and electrical equipment that need careful lifting, documentation and route planning.

  • Special Equipment
  • Air Freight

Steel & Metals

Iron, steel, pipes and fabricated structures moved in volume, as containers or breakbulk.

  • Ocean Freight
  • Breakbulk

Chemicals & Industrial Gases

Bulk liquids and chemicals in ISO tanks, with compatibility checks, IMDG documents and cleaning certificates.

  • ISO Tanks
  • NVOCC

Textiles & Apparel

Garments on hanger and fabric consignments, with value-added services such as kitting, tagging and barcoding.

  • Air Freight (GOH)
  • Value-Added

Food & Agri Products

Processed foods, edible oils and agri products, moved by sea or in food-grade tanks and refrigerated trucks.

  • Ocean Freight
  • ISO Tanks

Retail & E-commerce

Warehousing, distribution, last-mile delivery and reverse logistics for fast-moving consumer goods.

  • 3PL & Warehousing
  • Last-mile
